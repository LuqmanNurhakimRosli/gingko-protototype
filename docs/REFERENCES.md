# Reference systems: Japan, Singapore, Malaysia

The flood drill borrows proven patterns from public flood-warning and evacuation
systems. This page lists what each system does, what Ginkgo adopted, and what it
deliberately does not claim. Facts were checked in October 2026 through search
excerpts of the cited pages; open the key sources before quoting them.

## What Ginkgo adopted

| Pattern | From | In Ginkgo |
|---|---|---|
| 5 alert levels, each tied to one action | Japan, 警戒レベル (Cabinet Office, revised 2021) | Level 2 check your plan · 3 elderly and disabled leave · 4 everyone leaves · 5 flooding now (do not wait for it) |
| Fixed warning colours, look-ahead up to 3 h | JMA Kikikuru (キキクル) risk map | Map look-ahead: yellow ≤ 3 h, red ≤ 2 h, purple ≤ 1 h; flooded ground in blue; black badge for Level 5 |
| "Equivalent to" an alert, not an order | JMA notes on Kikikuru | Every drill screen says *Simulation · not a forecast*; the AI must say "in the drill" and point to official instructions |
| Shelters designated per hazard | Japan, 指定緊急避難場所 (GSI map) | Only centres on ground that stays Low/Moderate under Extreme rain are listed; none in High cells |
| Shelter crowding in 4 states, no app needed | VACAN (空いています / やや混雑 / 混雑 / 満) | Space available · filling up · crowded · full, on the map and every card |
| Space per person | Sphere Handbook 3.5 m² (adopted by Japan's guidelines, Dec 2024) | Centre capacity = usable floor area ÷ 3.5 m² |
| Personal timeline | Japan, My Timeline (マイ・タイムライン, MLIT) | "Leave by T+h:mm": the latest departure that still has a dry route out |
| Stage thresholds per station, rising/falling | Malaysia Public InfoBanjir (Normal / Waspada / Amaran / Bahaya); Singapore PUB sensors (50/75/90/100 %) | Not yet: the drill has no gauges. A next step is to drive it from live InfoBanjir levels |
| PPS in schools, halls and mosques | NADMA / JKM practice (8,481 PPS nationally, 2024) | Candidate centres are exactly those building types, from open data |
| People who stay, stranded residents | Taman Sri Muda, December 2021 | "No centre within reach" is counted and shown; routes warn when the start is already under water |

## Japan

- **Flood Kikikuru (洪水キキクル)** maps flood risk along small and medium rivers from the Basin Rainfall Index against warning criteria, looking up to 3 hours ahead and updated every 10 minutes. Colours: yellow 注意 (Level 2), red 警戒 (Level 3), purple 危険 (Level 4), black 災害切迫 (Level 5, since June 2022). JMA describes these as *level-equivalent* information.
  [JMA risk map](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/know/bosai/riskmap.html) · [JMA Kobe notice, 2022](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kobe-c/press/2022/2022063001/oshirase-kikikuru.pdf)
- **Evacuation alert levels (警戒レベル)**, revised May 2021: Level 3 高齢者等避難 (elderly and disabled evacuate), Level 4 避難指示 (everyone leaves dangerous places), Level 5 緊急安全確保 (secure your life; not always issued).
  [Cabinet Office guideline](https://www.bousai.go.jp/oukyu/hinanjouhou/r3_hinanjouhou_guideline/)
- **VACAN** shows shelter crowding in four states, updated by shelter staff and opened by URL or QR code; about 200 municipalities use it for shelters.
  [Nagasaki City](https://www.city.nagasaki.lg.jp/bousai/210004/214000/p036070.html)
- **Designated emergency evacuation sites (指定緊急避難場所)** are designated per hazard type (flood, landslide, tsunami…); GSI publishes them on its web map.
  [GSI](https://www.gsi.go.jp/bousaichiri/hinanbasho.html) · [Kasaneru Hazard Map](https://disaportal.gsi.go.jp/)
- **Shelter space:** national guidelines adopted Sphere in December 2024, with a minimum of 3.5 m² per person.
  [Cabinet Office, 2025](https://www.bousai.go.jp/taisaku/hinanjo/pdf/r7_0610.pdf)
- **My Timeline (マイ・タイムライン):** a household's own time-ordered plan of what to do as a river rises.
  [MLIT Kanto](https://www.ktr.mlit.go.jp/tonejo/tonejo00762.html)

## Singapore

- **PUB** runs more than 1,000 water-level sensors and more than 500 CCTVs (November 2025). SMS alerts go out at 50, 75, 90 and 100 % of canal depth, with an all-clear below 50 %; real-time flood information is in the myENV app.
  [PUB, 2025](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Resources/News-Room/PressReleases/2025/11/PUB-launches-second-flood-resilience-campaign-to-promote-community-preparedness) · [SMS alerts](https://www.pub.gov.sg/Public/KeyInitiatives/Flood-Resilience/Flood-Forecasting-and-Monitoring/Subscribe-to-SMS-Alerts)
- **NEA/MSS 2-hour nowcast** is open on data.gov.sg, updated every 30 minutes; **Virtual Singapore** is a 3D city model used, among other things, for flood analysis.
  [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_91ffc58263cff535910c16a4166ccbc3/view) · [GovTech](https://www.tech.gov.sg/technews/5-things-to-know-about-virtual-singapore/)

## Malaysia

- **Public InfoBanjir** (JPS/DID) shows river levels and rainfall every 15 minutes, with per-station thresholds Normal → Waspada → Amaran → Bahaya.
  [publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my](https://publicinfobanjir.water.gov.my) · [The Star, 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/18/four-selangor-river-monitoring-stations-breach-warning-level-says-did)
- **PRAB**, the national flood forecasting and warning programme, gives 7-day forecasts and 2-day warnings; hydrodynamic models for the Bernam, Selangor, Buloh, Klang and Langat basins were completed in 2025.
  [Malay Mail, 2025](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/09/20/irrigation-dept-defends-selangor-early-warning-system-pledges-stronger-flood-alerts/191761)
- **NADMA Portal Bencana / JKM InfoBencana** list open PPS and evacuees; nationally there are 8,481 PPS for about 2.16 million people (2024). Selangor PPS have included schools, community halls and mosques.
  [Utusan, 2024](https://www.utusan.com.my/berita/2024/09/8481-pps-muat-2-1j-bagi-mangsa-bah/) · [Utusan, 2021](https://www.utusan.com.my/nasional/komuniti/2021/12/banjir-17-pps-dibuka-di-klang-shah-alam/)
- **Taman Sri Muda, December 2021:** residents were stranded for days; many did not evacuate when the rain began; on 26 December 15,404 people were in 83 PPS across Selangor.
  [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2021/12/20/nadma-d-g-many-in-taman-sri-muda-refused-to-evacuate-when-rain-began-despit/2029875) · [NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2021/12/755907/taman-sri-muda-flood-victims-still-waiting-help)

## Method references

- **HAND (Height Above Nearest Drainage):** Nobre et al. 2011, *J. Hydrol.* 404:13–29, doi:10.1016/j.jhydrol.2011.03.051; Nobre et al. 2016, *Hydrol. Process.* 30:320–333, doi:10.1002/hyp.10581. NOAA OWP's HAND inundation mapping adds synthetic rating curves ([fact sheet](https://www.weather.gov/media/owp/operations/SRC_Factsheet.pdf)).
- **Sphere Handbook 2018:** minimum 3.5 m² covered living space per person, excluding cooking and washing space ([Sphere](https://spherestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Sphere-in-Practice.pdf)).

## What Ginkgo does not claim

- The drill is not a forecast. It has no live rainfall, river-gauge or hydraulic model; it replays a scenario on the susceptibility grid so people can rehearse. Real 1-hour warnings need rainfall nowcasts (MET Malaysia radar), river levels (InfoBanjir) and a calibrated model such as PRAB's.
- Evacuation centres are candidates from open data with estimated capacity, not the official NADMA/JKM list. Official instructions always take precedence.
